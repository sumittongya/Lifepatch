User Guide & SIH Jury Walkthrough

Step-by-step verification flows, rural usability instructions, and technical architecture specs.

What is LIFE PATCH? SIH26133

LIFE PATCH is a patient-centric, rural-first digital health record platform engineered for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement SIH26133). It bridges rural citizens, frontline ASHA workers, Primary Health Centres (PHCs), and specialist referral hospitals into an integrated, consent-governed longitudinal health record that functions with 100% offline resilience.

Demonstration Steps for Evaluators & Juries
  1. Patient Multilingual Voice Entry: Go to Patient Portal → Report Symptoms → Speak or select a sample phrase in Hindi, Telugu, or English → Save to EHR.
  2. Longitudinal Health Timeline: View the patient's lifetime timeline to see the real-time record addition with verified status.
  3. Doctor Priority Triage & Queue: Switch to Doctor Workspace → Check the live OPD priority queue with wait times and override priority.
  4. Clinical Review & e-Prescriptions: Open the patient chart → Review grounded clinical AI summary → Issue an e-prescription with drug allergy checking and print the official slip.
  5. District Referral Transfer: Create a referral to District Hospital Cardiology with encrypted diagnostic sharing.
  6. ASHA Field Visit & Offline Sync: Switch to ASHA Worker → Record household vitals → Simulate offline storage → Sync to PHC with 1 click.
Data Privacy & Architecture Guarantees
  • 100% Client-Side IndexedDB Engine: 17 isolated data stores guarantee zero data loss without requiring external cloud databases.
  • ABDM-Aligned ABHA Identifiers: Standardized 14-digit ABHA IDs with consent gatekeeping.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Clinical AI: AI assists summarization and triage but clinical responsibility remains with licensed doctors.
